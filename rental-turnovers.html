Rental turnover painting

We work with landlords and property managers to get vacant units ready to rent again. Between tenants, we patch the wear, prime the repairs and stains, and repaint the walls and ceilings so the unit shows clean and fresh.

What’s included in a turnover

  • PrimingPatches and marks primed so they don’t show through
  • WallsRepainted in your standard colour
  • CeilingsFreshened up so the whole room looks finished

Your written quote spells out exactly what’s included. You get one flat price based on the unit size and the scope we agree on, so there are no surprises on the bill.
