Questions

Frequently asked questions

How long does a rental turnover usually take?

Most rental turnovers take 2–4 days, depending on the scope. We agree on a finish date before we start, and if we miss it you get $100 off.

How do you price a rental unit?

You get one flat price based on the unit size and the scope we agree on. The written quote spells out exactly what’s included, so you know the full cost before we start.

Can you work around move-in dates and showings?

Yes. Tell us the move-in date or any showings when you ask for a quote and we’ll set the finish date around them. Evening and weekend work helps us fit in.

Can I get a quote by sending photos?

Yes. Send a few photos, the location and what needs painting, and we’ll come back with a quote or let you know if we need to see it in person first.

Do you paint occupied homes?

Yes. We paint rooms, hallways, ceilings, trim and doors in lived-in homes. We protect your floors and furniture and clean up the work area before we leave.

Do you work evenings and weekends?

Yes, evenings and weekends are available when that works better around your household, tenants or showings.
